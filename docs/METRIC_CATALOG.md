# Metric catalog

| Metric | Grain | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| Complaints received | Complaint | Distinct official complaint records in the window |
| Timely response | Complaint | CFPB timely flag divided by complaints |
| Narrative coverage | Complaint | Published non-empty narratives divided by complaints |
| Monetary relief share | Complaint | Responses classified as monetary relief divided by complaints |
| Company-product signal | Company-product | Operational metrics shown only with at least 25 records |

Raw volume is never presented as a complaint rate or company quality score.
